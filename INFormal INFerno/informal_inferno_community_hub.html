Our Code of Conduct

THE NON-TOXIC PLEDGE

Every INFormal INFerno warrior stands by four core pillars before entering battle.

01

Respect All Players

Win with modesty, lose with dignity, and treat allies and opponents with grace.

02

Zero Gatekeeping

Help newcomers learn game mechanics and decks without judgment or ego.

03

Good Game Spirit

Celebrate great plays from both sides. No emote spam or toxic taunting allowed.

04

Constructive Vibe

Offer encouraging feedback and tips. Leave frustration and blame at the door.
